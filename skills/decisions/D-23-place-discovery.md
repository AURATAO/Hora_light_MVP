# D-23: Place discovery in the text channel (phase 1)

**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Status:** Proposed; items 8–11 revised 2026-11-05. Built on `feat/discovery`, off unless `CHANNEL_DISCOVERY=1`. Not merged, not deployed.
**Trigger:** Aura, 2026-11-04: customers should be able to ask HO:RA to find things in NYC, and every answer should lead to an errand HO:RA can do.

## Decision
A customer can ask the text channel to find something: a place for an errand, what is open now, where to get an item, a gift, a return drop-off, food worth waiting in line for, a route for several errands, someone to go and check, or "the usual". The model proposes and the rules decide, as in D-18:

1. **The model never writes a place.** Sonnet (`LLM_AGENT_MODEL`) runs a tool loop over Google Places (New) Text Search (`search_places`, at most `CHANNEL_PLACES_PER_MESSAGE` calls). It returns one structured answer that names places only by reference (`p1`, `p2`) to this turn's results. The router renders every name, address, rating and opening status from Google's data (`RenderDiscovery`). An "info" answer with no search shows nothing.
2. **An info reply never sets up a YES.** It never quotes a price and never promises a time. The model's only free text is a one-line lead. `InfoLeadOK` refuses any lead with money words, a question or "yes/book/confirm", a time promise, rating figures, a link or a phone number; a refused lead is replaced with fixed text. The reply ends with "Reply with a number".
3. **A pick is a normal draft.** "2", "the second one" or "Joe's" is matched by rule, and the model's `choose` handles a pick that comes with more words. The pick goes through `startDraftOpts`: parse, NYC area check, budget rules, summary and plain YES, all unchanged. The chosen place is attached after the parse by rule (`applyPlaces`). The parser reads the person's own words plus Google's address, never the business name. A name can therefore never become a money input (gap C, `ownwords.go`).
4. **Check errands** ("does Trader Joe's still have X?") book at 15 minutes, the base fee's included time, unless the person names a length. The summary says the supporter will check and text back with a photo. Any purchase still needs a budget.
5. **"The usual"** only uses the person's own posted or completed tasks. It takes the title that repeats most, otherwise the newest. It is offered as a new draft with no parse; a past budget is never carried (amounts are stripped), and the summary still needs YES.
6. **Out of scope** (general chat, medical or legal advice, alcohol, tobacco or cannabis, prescriptions, account logins or reservations, price guarantees) gets fixed copy per reason that names what HO:RA can do.
7. **Failure is fixed copy.** If Anthropic, Places or the daily cap fails, the reply is "I can't search right now, but tell me the store or address and I'll send someone" when the words asked to search by rule. Otherwise the message goes to the usual routing. GLM cannot run the tool loop, so discovery is Anthropic only.
8. **Only place ids are stored.** `channel_discovery` (RLS on, no grants, no policies) keeps the open offer's place ids, the person's words and the daily count. A chosen place is looked up again (Place Details Pro). A pick by name ("Joe's", "the bakery") is matched against names looked up again by id (Place Details, `displayName` only), never against the message log. Each name lookup counts against `CHANNEL_PLACES_PER_DAY`. A number or ordinal needs no lookup.
9. **"Looking that up…"** is sent once, by fixed copy, just before a turn's first Places search (the answer takes 6–8 s). It is never sent for an answer with no search.
10. **A search while a booking summary is open** runs discovery only when the words can only be a search (`IsClearSearch`: "find me a…", "where can I get…", "any … near", "is there a … open"). The draft is left as it is, and every such reply ends with "Your earlier booking is still waiting." (no YES in it). A pick from those options is not booked over the open draft: the person is asked to answer that one first. "Make it 4pm" still re-drafts. A model answer that is not a search's (not_discovery, a pick, the usual) falls back to the draft path.
11. **Several errands with no place** ("pick up dry cleaning, buy a birthday card, mail a package"; `IsPlacelessErrandList`, rule only, after the supporter routing) get a fixed question: "Want me to find places for these on one route, or do you have the addresses?" "Find them" (or a yes) runs route planning with the errands as earlier words. A no drops the question. Anything else is taken as the addresses and goes down the normal draft path with the errands.
12. **The requester agent gets an `info` action** (prompt v0.4.6). It is used only with nothing of ours open. With a supporter on another job, `info` runs discovery, and "not discovery" falls back to the supporter (P7).

### Follow-ups from the first real test (production 2026-11-06 00:09–00:11 Rome; prompt d0.3)
13. **Speed.** "my office is 250 Mercer street can you recommend me few nice pizzeria?" took 21 s, and "Looking that up…" went out only after 11 s (GLM classify 3.7 s plus Sonnet's first call 4.7 s). The same query ran twice. Now:
    - A search by rule (`LooksLikeDiscovery`) goes to discovery **before** the classifier. The classifier runs only if the answer is not_discovery.
    - "Looking that up…" is sent before the first model call when the words say where to look (`placeGivenRE`, or the answer to "Where should I look?"), and only if the daily cap still allows a search (`PlacesCallsLeft`, which counts nothing). Otherwise it is sent at the first search, as before. It goes out at most once per turn.
    - Places results are cached per turn by normalised query plus `open_now`. A repeated query is answered from the first result: no Google call, no count.
    - **Search once, then answer.** Once a round of searches has results, the tool is switched off and the next call answers. A round with an empty search may search again. The prompt asks for every search in the first reply.
14. **A pick by name inside a longer message** ("Ok nolita pizza, what is the best flavor?") is that option, by rule, with no model call (`pickNameIn`). The name may be the full name, its core before " - " or "(", or its first two or more words; one word only when it is distinctive and at least five letters. Two options named, or "not / instead / than / or / cheaper…", is no pick, and the model reads it. A question asked with the pick is kept as a supporter note on the draft (`Notes`, shown in the summary and sent with the booking). The intro says HO:RA has no menu.
15. **Questions about the options** ("which one should I pick?", "what's good there?", "do they do delivery?", "No I was asking which pizza should I choose") are read by discovery before the classifier whenever options are open. The new action is `about`, with a topic (pick, menu, delivery, other), the option asked about, and an item word. The reply is fixed copy built from Google's data, looked up again by id (Place Details Enterprise: rating, reviews, price level in words, hours). No menu item is ever named, and the model writes no text here. Its item word ("pizza") is used only if `AboutItemOK` accepts it. The rule suggests the option asked about, or else the best rated. "Want me to set that up at X?": a YES starts that option's draft, whose summary still asks its own YES before booking. The options stay open, so numbers still pick, and "no" / "never mind" drops them. A new errand ("also pick up my dry cleaning at 100 Washington St") comes back not_discovery and drafts as before. A long message gets one set of lookups, shared by the name match and the answer.

## Context and alternatives
- **Model-written replies, with places from its own text.** Rejected. A rating or an opening hour the model misremembers is stated as fact, and the reply could carry a price or a YES question.
- **Storing names and addresses for the pick.** Rejected. Google's Places policy permits storing place ids only, so the pick is looked up again ($0.017 a stop).
- **Google attribution, as found 2026-11-04** (developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies). Where Places content is shown without a Google map, the text "Google Maps" must appear, unchanged and untranslated, near the content and visually separate from it. Authors must be credited for any review or photo shown. We show neither reviews nor photos, nor request them. The policy is written for screens and says nothing about SMS. Our reply ends with a separate line: "Places, ratings and hours from Google Maps. Hours can change."

## Evidence
- 2026-11-06, prompt d0.3, `go run ./cmd/agenttest -discovery -runs 3` (53 cases; 9 new from the production test; fixture places): **159 PASS**, 0 FAIL, 0 UNSAFE, 0 ERROR. An answer with options made **2.14 model calls** on average (71 of 83 took 2, 12 took 3) and 1.25 searches. Latency: 4.6 s median, 7.8 s p95, with the classifier no longer in front. The pizza request took 2 calls and 1 search in 4.3–4.4 s. An `about` answer took 1 call, about 2 s. Before (main, same 44 old cases, 1 run): 1.18 searches per case; after: 0.77.
- `go run ./cmd/agenttest -discovery` (38 cases; Sonnet 5.5; fixture places; 3 runs): 113 PASS, 1 FAIL, 0 UNSAFE, 0 ERROR. The miss is #38.1, "wash and fold near Irving Place", answered with "Where should I look?" (safe). One raw lead in 114 was refused by rule before the rule was narrowed. No reply carried a YES, a price or a forbidden place.
- Requester agent, prompt v0.4.6, all 171 cases: 165 PASS, 3 SAME, 2 FAIL, 1 UNSAFE. #9, #38 and #105 fail identically on `main` in 3/3 runs: #38 is the known "the $10 is fine", and #9 and #105 are decided by rules in production. All 8 new `info` cases passed.
- Cost from that run: an answer with options averaged $0.0076 of Sonnet plus 2.08 Text Search Enterprise calls ($0.073). Each other answer averaged $0.0015. A discovery conversation that books is about $0.08 plus $0.017 per chosen stop, before Google's 1,000 free Enterprise searches a month. Latency: 5.9 s on average and 8.3 s at p95 for an answer with options.
